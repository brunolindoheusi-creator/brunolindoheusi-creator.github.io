Common questions

Where is my village saved?

On your device. The game saves by itself as you play, and again whenever you leave it. Removing the game from your device removes the save with it.

Does the game need the internet or an account?

No. It plays fully offline and has no account, no sign-in and no ads.

How do I see what a building or a spell does?

Hold your finger on its card, or tap the small "i" on a building's card. Tap anywhere else to close the details.

Can I change the speed, or pause?

Yes. The pause button and the 1×, 2× and 3× speed buttons are at the bottom right of the battlefield.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Menu, then Settings, then Language. The game is in English and 30 other languages.

How do I start over?

Open Menu, then Settings, then "Erase all progress". This cannot be undone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store you bought the game from. For the App Store, Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
