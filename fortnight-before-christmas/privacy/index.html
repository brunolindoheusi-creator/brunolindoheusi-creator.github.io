Fortnight Before Christmas

Privacy Policy · in effect from 16 November 2026

In short: the game collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not go online.

Who this is from

Fortnight Before Christmas is made and published by Bruno Heusi ("I", "me"). This policy covers the game on every device it is sold for.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game:

  • has no account and no sign-in;
  • shows no ads;
  • has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers;
  • does not connect to the internet;
  • does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

What stays on your device

Your village, your progress and your settings (such as language and sound volume) are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They are never sent to me. Removing the game removes them.

If your device or the store you bought the game from offers backups or similar services, your save may be included in them. Those services are run by their own providers under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

The store you buy the game from handles the payment. I never see your payment details.

If you write to me

If you send an email to the support address, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use them only to answer you, and I do not pass them on to anyone.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text and its date will be published on this page.
